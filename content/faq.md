## What is the SSB interview?

The Services Selection Board (SSB) is a 5-day assessment process used by the Indian Armed Forces to select candidates for commissioned officer roles. It combines intelligence tests, psychology tests, group tasks, and a personal interview to assess leadership potential and Officer Like Qualities (OLQs), not just academic knowledge.

## How many days does SSB take?

SSB runs across 5 days: Day 1 is Screening (OIR and PPDT), Day 2 is Psychology Tests (TAT, WAT, SRT, SD), Days 3-4 are GTO Tasks, and Day 5 covers the remaining Interviews and the Conference where results are announced.

## What happens if I don't clear screening on Day 1?

Candidates who do not clear the OIR and PPDT screening on Day 1 are informed the same day and sent home -- they do not continue to the remaining four days. Screening exists specifically to filter the intake before the more resource-intensive stages begin.

## How many times can I attempt SSB?

There is no fixed universal limit on the number of SSB attempts across a candidate's eligible age and qualification window -- many recommended officers succeeded only on their second or third attempt. Each entry scheme (NDA, CDS, AFCAT, TES, etc.) has its own eligibility and age-based windows, so check the specific scheme's rules for how many times you can apply within your eligible period.

## What are Officer Like Qualities (OLQs)?

OLQs are a fixed set of 15 personality traits -- grouped under Planning & Organizing, Social Adjustment, Social Effectiveness, and Dynamic qualities -- that every SSB task is designed to reveal. Assessors build a picture of these traits from patterns repeated across Psychology tests, GTO tasks, and the Interview, rather than scoring any single test in isolation.

## Is the SSB interview only about being confident and outgoing?

No. Confidence helps, but assessors are specifically trained to distinguish genuine, consistent behavior from a rehearsed or overconfident performance. Quieter candidates who contribute practical, well-reasoned input in group tasks and give honest, specific interview answers are assessed just as favorably as naturally outgoing ones.

## What is the Conference at the end of SSB?

The Conference, held on Day 5, is where the President, Interviewing Officer, Group Testing Officer, and Psychologist come together to review every candidate's performance across all five days and reach a final recommendation decision. It is a review and cross-verification stage, not a new test -- most of your outcome is already shaped by your performance in the preceding four days.

## What happens after I'm recommended at SSB?

Recommended candidates proceed to a medical examination (typically 4-5 days) at a designated military hospital, which checks fitness against Armed Forces medical standards. Your final merit position is confirmed only after clearing this medical examination.

## How should I prepare for SSB?

Read up on each stage individually (OIR, PPDT, Psychology, GTO, Interview, Conference), practice under realistic time limits (SSB's tests are almost always more about time pressure than raw difficulty), and spend time reflecting honestly on your own strengths, weaknesses, and motivations -- consistency across all five days matters more than any single strong performance.
